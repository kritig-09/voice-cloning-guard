# SIH Submission Guide

Use this repository for the Social Innovation Hackathon (SIH) submission package.

## Required presentation structure

1. Title
2. Proposed Solution
3. Technical Approach
4. Feasibility & Viability
5. Impact & Benefits
6. Research & References

## Placeholders to replace

- `[Team Name]`
- `[Institution]`
- `[Member 1]`
- `[Member 2]`
- `[Member 3]`
- `[Demo URL]`
- `[Presentation URL]`

## Recommended claims

Describe the software as a prototype and distinguish measured evaluation from planned future work. Do not quote an accuracy number unless the evaluation protocol and dataset are documented.
